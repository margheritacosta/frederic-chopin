My name is **Margherita** and I am an Italian student with a deep admiration for the linguistic and cultural richness of my country. Having grown up in Italy, I have a deep appreciation for its vast historical and artistic heritage, as well as the diversity of its dialects, which I consider to be an essential part of social identity.

My studies in _'Language, Society and Communication'_ aim to improve my language skills and explore how language and its dynamics shape society and promote intercultural growth. 

Alongside my passion for words, I am also passionate about music. I have a deep interest in the **piano**, which I consider to be a universal form of communication. For me, notes, just like words, are a powerful vehicle for expressing emotion.
